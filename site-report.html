Notes

How to read these numbers

Source: Google Analytics 4, property bushwickdaily.com, queried August 13, 2026 for the trailing 90 days (May 15 – August 12). Users are GA4 unique users; pageviews are GA4 screenPageViews.

Geography: New York City is the largest reader city at 53,971 users. GA4 leaves 31,514 users' locations unset, and international traffic (some of it automated, as on any public news site) makes up part of the remainder. For planning a Brooklyn campaign, the NYC figure is the conservative floor of the local audience.

Channels beyond the site: Instagram @bushwickdaily, 45,767 followers; daily newsletter, ~15,700 subscribers with 30% opens on recent dedicated sends.

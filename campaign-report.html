Notes

How to read these numbers

Two open rates are shown on purpose. Apple Mail auto-loads emails on Apple's servers whether or not a person reads them, which inflates every sender's raw open rate. 30.0% is the industry-standard figure (what every media kit quotes); 8.5% is the floor after excluding those machine opens. The truth is between them, and the comparable send is measured the same way on both.
